# KUDOS posts (each < 280 chars)

## 1. What it is
KUDOS launches pump.fun coins for someone else's X account. 90% of the coin's creator fees are credited to that account; 10% buys back and burns $KUDOS. No launch fee. [link]

## 2. What people gain
Got a coin launched for you? Post your Solana address tagging @kudoslaunch, paste the link on KUDOS, and your fee balance is sent to that wallet. No sign-up. A coin can earn very little; buying any coin can lose you money. [link]

## 3. How it works
Every trade pays a pump.fun creator fee. Every 10 min KUDOS reads them per coin: 90% to the recipient's vault, 10% to a bucket that buys $KUDOS on Jupiter and burns it. Every payout and burn is on-chain, listed with its tx. [link]
